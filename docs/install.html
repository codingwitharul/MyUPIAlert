Installation Guide

Get your free offline Voice Soundbox running in less than 2 minutes.

1 Download the App

Because MyUPIAlert intercepts system notifications and SMS to function as a soundbox, it cannot be hosted on the Google Play Store (due to their SMS permission policies). You must download the APK directly from our open-source repository.

Download Latest APK

2 Install the APK

Once downloaded, tap the APK file to install it. You may see a prompt saying "Install unknown apps".

  • Tap Settings on the prompt.
  • Toggle on Allow from this source.
  • Go back and tap Install.

3 Grant Required Permissions

When you open the app for the first time, you will see a prominent red banner asking for permissions. These are strictly required for the app to function:

  • SMS Permissions: Allows the app to read banking transactional messages.
  • Notification Access: Allows the app to read incoming pushes from Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm.
Privacy Guarantee: All processing is done 100% locally on your device using SQLite. We do not have any cloud servers, and your data never leaves your phone.

4 Disable Battery Optimization (Crucial)

To ensure the app always speaks aloud even when your screen is off, you must exclude it from Android's aggressive battery killers.

  • The app will prompt you to "Ignore Battery Optimization". Tap Allow.
  • For Xiaomi/Oppo/Vivo users: You must also manually go to Settings > Apps > MyUPIAlert and enable Auto-Start.

5 Select Language & Test

Go to the Settings tab, select your preferred regional language (e.g., Hindi, Tamil), adjust the volume to 200%, and press Test Default Sound.

You are now ready to receive payments!
