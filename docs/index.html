Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions merchants ask before switching to MyUPIAlert.

Do I need an active internet connection or SIM card on the soundbox phone? ▼
No! If you place a phone with your registered bank SIM card, MyUPIAlert parses incoming bank SMS fully offline without requiring any internet or data plan. If you use Wi-Fi or mobile data, push notifications from PhonePe, Google Pay, and Paytm work instantly as well.
Can I connect the phone to an external Bluetooth speaker? ▼
Yes. MyUPIAlert includes a Bluetooth Keep-Alive feature that prevents counter speakers from entering sleep mode, ensuring the first second of speech is never clipped.
What happens if both an app notification and an SMS arrive for the same payment? ▼
MyUPIAlert features an intelligent Cross-Channel Deduplication engine. The first alert is announced aloud, and the companion alert within 3 minutes is silently absorbed. It will never repeat the announcement or double-count your revenue.
Is my financial transaction data safe and private? ▼
100% safe. MyUPIAlert is open-source under the MIT license and contains zero external HTTP client libraries, zero analytics SDKs, and zero cloud backends. All data stays strictly in local SQLite storage on your phone.
Are there any hidden costs, in-app purchases, or expiry dates? ▼
None. It is completely free and open-source forever. You can freely install it across 1 counter or 100 counters without paying a single rupee.
